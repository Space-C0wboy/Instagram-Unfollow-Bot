Instagram Cleanup

Read this before you start

This tool automates your Instagram account, which is against Instagram's Terms of Use.

It drives a real browser to visit profiles and press Unfollow or Follow for you. Instagram's rules forbid automated access. Accounts that do this can be temporarily restricted or permanently disabled, and there is no way to guarantee that will not happen to yours.

The tool goes slowly and stops at a daily limit to keep that risk low, it never touches an account you did not tick, and every unfollow is written to a backup you can restore from. The risk is still yours.

The terms are at help.instagram.com/581066165581870.

Almost there

Log in to Instagram in the window that just opened. This screen will move on by itself once you are in.

Welcome

What this does. It looks through everyone you follow on Instagram, finds the accounts that have not posted in over a year, and shows you the list. Nothing is unfollowed until you say so.

Backups. Before any unfollowing starts, a backup of those accounts is saved. If you change your mind later, Settings has a Restore button that lets you follow them again.

It takes a while, on purpose. Instagram blocks accounts that unfollow too many people too fast, so this tool works slowly and stops at about 100 a day. Leave it running, close it whenever you like, and it picks up where it left off. While it works, leave the Instagram window alone: the tool is using it.
